Irani Café

EST. 1953  ·  CAMP, PUNE

The counter, the stock book and the staff register, on one screen.

Chai goes out in ninety seconds. The bill, the stock count and the shift record should keep up. This system replaces the four paper registers the café runs on today.

From our field visit and interview with the café manager. Every row on the right is a screen in the system.

"We write everything in a register — name, phone, joining date, work role."Manager, on staff records
Staff register holds the same fields, searchable, with no second copy to keep.
"Attendance is marked on paper, but sometimes staff forget to sign."Manager, on attendance
One tap marks a shift. The month sheet totals itself.
"We divide morning, afternoon and evening shifts manually."Manager, on rostering
Shift chart shows who is on the floor, per shift, per day.
"If someone takes extra leave it is cut manually. Overtime is added by hand."Manager, on payroll
Salary is computed from days present and overtime hours. No arithmetic by hand.

Five modules

Counter

Take the order, add items, settle the bill by cash or UPI.

Bills

Every bill of the day, reopenable and printable.

Stock

Milk, tea, bun, kheema. Falls as orders are billed.

Staff register

Records, shifts, attendance and monthly salary.

Reports

Day and week sales, top items, payment split.
